To derive the energy levels, we start with the time-independent Schrödinger equation:

$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2\psi(x)}{dx^2} = E\psi(x)$

For a particle in a 1D box with infinite potential walls at $x=0$ and $x=L$, the wavefunction $\psi(x)$ must vanish at these boundaries. This leads to the quantization of the wave vector $k$:

$k = \frac{n\pi}{L}, \quad n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$

Substituting this back into the energy expression gives the quantized energy levels:

$E_n = \frac{n^2 h^2}{8mL^2}$
